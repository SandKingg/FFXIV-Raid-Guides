heat burst - raidwide

fireflight by pyrelight/emberlight - jumps north, indicating a cleave on one side of her, then three lines showing which directions she will move across the arena
	the cleave will follow her, so stay on the correct sides of all the flights
	the first dash will be north-south, then diagonally from south to either east or west, then horizontally east-west or vice-versa
	if the second dash goes away from the side that was just cleaved, that's the good pattern
		just stay slightly south, then move towards the cardinal she'll end up at
	if the second dash goes into the side that was just cleaved, that's the bad pattern
		try to stay towards the far south corner on the side that she'll end up on
		then cross the east-west line after the second dash
sun circlet - a quick doughnut aoe around her so make sure to end up close to her
	if the previous cast was pyrelight, there'll be a stack after this
	if the previous cast was ember light, there'll be spreads on each player after this

wheel of fableflight - summons four clones towards the four corners that each indicate a cleave and which way they will rotate
	find a safe spot that will not be hit by any of the cleaves
kindled flame/scattered kindling - either a stack on both supports or both dps, or a spread aoe on every player
	for pairs have the melee pair stand closer to the boss
	for spreads, assign corner-relative positions to spread to

fire of victory - an aoe tankbuster

fireflight: four long nights - indicates a cleave on one side of her along with a close/far marker and the direction she will rotate in four times
	stay on the correct half of the room to avoid being cleaved each time
	one player each time needs to be either close or far to take a baited aoe
		each bait gives a vuln so each player will take one bait throughout the mechanic
	the rest of the party should be exactly on the hitbox to take a stack together
	there will always be two close and two far baits, and you will always need to swap sides twice and stay once

pari's curse - a raidwide that gives an array of debuffs to players
	two players will get spreads and one player gets a stack
	either both spread players, or one spread and the two players in the stack will get fire debuffs that must be cleansed by getting hit by ice
fableflight - a clone appear in the NE corner that cleaves either to her left or right, making one half of the arena unsafe
charming baubles - creates an ice bauble that drops down behind her
	two fire baubles will drop down later
thieves' weave - wraps the ice bauble in a rug and creates two more rugs to act as decoys
	these rugs jump three times then reveal their contents
	the fire baubles drop down here as well, but they always land on the NE-SW diagonal, and always with one tile between them
	three tiles will be safe, two of which are hit by ice and two of which are only half safe due to the clone's cleave
	try to find a correct tile with both spreads being in different tiles

spurning flames - a raidwide that empowers the boss
impassioned sparks - spawns four sets of four expanding aoes, centred on tiles, and gives two players alpha debuffs and two players beta debuffs
	there will be four sets of large point-blank aoes baited on two players that leave behind persistent puddles
	these need to be placed on cardinals, so find the empty first cardinal and then rotate either CW or ACW depending on the aoes
burning chains - the alpha players are chained together and the beta players are chained together
	make sure to stack mid before the chains appear to minimise the distance to break them
hot foot - a massive spread aoe on each player
	spread towards assigned cardinals, but don't go back to mid until the chains break
scouring scorn - a raidwide that disempowers the boss and removes the puddles

doubling - creates four clones towards the four cardinals
fableflight - each clone will indicate a cleave towards the left or right and tether to the closest player
	two players have shorter timers and two players have longer timers, indicating when their clone will turn towards them and do its cleave
	two sets of four towers will appear, with each cardinal eventually having two towers on the two middle tiles along its edge
	the shorter timers need to take the tower that turns their clone so the cleave points out of the arena
	the other two players then take the other two towers
	then swap responsibilities for the second set

kindled flame - three fire baubles have dropped down, with two being in the same row or column
	then two stack markers appear on two random players
	assign a priority for who stacks with who, and where

charméd flight: four long nights - indicates a cleave on one side of her along with the direction she will rotate in four times
	each indicator is accompanied by two fire baubles dropping down
	these ones are offset from the centres of the tiles by half
	stay in the correct half of the safe tile each time to dodge the cleaves and line aoes
