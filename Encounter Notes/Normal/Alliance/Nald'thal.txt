as above, so below - a raidwide that transitions the arena
heat above, flames below - splits the arena into a gold circle underneath the boss and blue everywhere else, and also changes the skybox to blue or gold
	whichever colour the sky is indicates which part of the floor will explode
	this one will always be a doughnut

as above, so below
heat above, flames below - the sky switches to the other colour, doing the opposite aoe
	this one will always be a point-blank

heavens' trial - marks three random players for cone aoes, and gives a stack marker to another player
	everyone not taking a cone should stack

golden tenet - a triple-stack tankbuster

as above, so below
far above, deep below - indicates a golden line stack on a random player, and three blue following aoes on other random players, and also changes the skybox to blue or gold
	whichever colour the sky is indicates which mechanic will actually happen
	beware, it may change partway through the cast

as above, so below
once above, ever below - creates four lines of three double-exaflares each towards the cardinals and changes the skybox to blue or gold
	two adjacent lines are gold and the other two are blue
	whichever colour the sky is indicates which colour of exaflares will go off

hell of fire - a half-room cleave on the side indicated by the glowing eyes

wayward soul - summons four waves of three large balls from the sky
	these balls do giant point-blank aoes where they land, so be careful
wayward soul - summons seven large balls in a spiral around the arena
	move parallel to them as they explode to avoid being hit

hell of fire - this time the bosses switch, doing the half-room cleave on the other side to where was initially shown

fired up i - indicates a large knockback at the back of the arena
fired up ii - indicates a large point-blank from either NE or NW
fortune's flux - jumps to each of the marked points, doing the aoes in order
	use the knockback to dodge the aoe, or just use knockback resist

fired up i - indicates a large point-blank in the middle of the arena
fired up ii - indicates a large knockback somewhere in the arena
fired up iii - indicates either a large knockback or large point-blank in another part of the arena
fortune's flux - this time dodge the aoe from mid before running to the knockback
	then either get knocked towards the second knockback or away from the second point-blank

soul's measure - splits the arena into a gold and blue half, and summons three soul vessels, starting an add phase
	equal weight - each vessel tethers to one alliance, allowing them to damage it
		it also takes on the appearance of a random alliance member
	twingaze - a cone aoe from each soul vessel towards a random player
	magmatic spell - a stack marker on a random alliance member from each soul vessel's alliance
balance - projects spectral scales at the front of the arena showing the balance of weights on each side
	the weight is determined by the number of players and soul vessel corpses on each side
	soul vessel corpses weigh more than players
	there's a bit of leeway but make sure the scales are balanced at the end of the cast otherwise you wipe
	doing this correctly gives everyone a damage up buff
