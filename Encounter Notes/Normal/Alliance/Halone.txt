rain of spears - a multi-hit raidwide

tetrapagos - telegraphs a point-blank aoe, a doughnut aoe, a left cleave and a right cleave in a random order
	these aoes then go off quickly in the order they were telegraphed
	this first one is always:
		point-blank/doughnut
		left/right
		doughnut/point-blank - opposite of first
		right/left - opposite of second

tetrapagos - this one now has the two cleaves telegraphed back to back as the second and third aoes, needing quicker dodging between the,

doom spear - creates three stack towers at NE, NW and south
	split into alliances to fill these
spears three - aoe tankbusters on each tank

thousandfold thrust - creates many red reticles that converge towards a point, then does a rapid multi-hit half-room cleave in that direction
thousandfold thrust

lochos - creates two sets of spears that fly through the arena
	one set flies horizontally, the other set flies vertically, leaving only one quadrant safe
	these spears hit many times so make sure to dodge as quickly as possible

will of the fury - creates five doughnut aoes that propagate inward from the edges of the arena
	getting hit freezes you in place, so be very careful
wrath of halone - a big proximity aoe from the middle
	dodge between two sets of doughnuts and move to the edge to minimise damage

add phase - three glacial spears spawn at NE, NW and south, plus one more in mid
	split into alliances and stand in the safe zones to attack each spear
	the cages around each spear disappear when it is destroyed, allowing you to attack the other spears	

	niphas + ice dart - point-blank aoes from each spear, plus spread aoes on random players

	cheimon - three line aoes from the middle spear that rotate in the direction indicated
