abundance - raidwide

matron's plenty - transforms the arena into pink or green and creates a small death wall around the edge
the giving land - a point-blank or doughnut aoe depending on the arena type
	spring flowers (pink) - point-blank
	summer shade (green) - doughnut

season's passing - the arena transforms to the other colour
the giving land - opposite of the previous

matron's harvest - a raidwide that returns the arena to normal

reaper's gale - two sets of checkerboard aoes that go off in the order they appear
	dodge diagonally from one into another

matron's plenty 2
floral haze - gives everyone a forced march debuff
the giving land - this goes off BEFORE the forced march, so dodge first, then do forced march

season's passing
the giving land - opposite of the previous, use your forced march to dodge this one
matron's harvest

matron's breath - creates two patches of flowers, one blue and one yellow, then does three aoes that are either blue or yellow
	stand in the matching colour of flower to avoid damage, and even get a tiny bit of healing
