katarraktes - raidwide + bleed

rheognosis - summons a clone towards the east or west that eventually does a big knockback from that side

thlipsis - a stack marker on a random player

left/right bank - a half-room cleave on the side indicated by the cast name
right/left bank - opposite of the previous

hydroptosis - spread markers on random players
	these give vulns so be very careful not to overlap

rhyton - line aoe tankbusters on each tank

tetraktys - transforms the arena into a large triangle made up of 9 smaller triangles
	three sets of three small triangles will start glowing in sequence, doing aoes when they reach full size
	then there will be three bigger triangles (made of 4 smaller triangles) to dodge
tetraktuos kosmos - one of the smaller triangles will have 3 rectangles rise up from its edges before falling straight down on the arena
	go directly opposite its tip to dodge
tetraktuos kosmos - this time two triangles create the aoes
	the other 'tip' small triangle away from both aoe triangles will be mostly safe
	the arena returns to normal after this

rheognosis petrine - does a rheognosis but includes two water orbs that get knocked back through the arena as well
	don't stand on the water orb side

hieroglyphica - turns the arena into a 4x4 grid of squares with two squares being safe
	the safe spots will always be a corner tile and a non-corner edge tile
	the grid then rotates 90 degrees in the indicated direction so stand where the safe spot will end up
