solar wings
sun's shine - creates three mirages of azeyma that do wind aoes, pushing the sunstorms towards the middle
	go next to one of the sunstorms that will be pushed to avoid the point blanks

solar fans - throws out her fans to the left and right, doing line aoes
radiant rhythm - rotates the fans around the arena either 4 or 5 times, each time rotating by 90 degrees
radiant finish - causes the fans to explode in massive point-blank aoes
	stay very close to her front/back or to her left/right to dodge

tankbusters

fleeting spark - turns to a random player, doing a 270-degree cleave in front of her

solar fold - a plus aoe from her that creates four slowly encroaching lines of fire
	these eventually split the arena into four quadrants
sun's shine - creates two mirages that jump to two diagonally opposite quadrants
	the wind aoes they do will push the fire into the other two quadrants, so stand in a quadrant with a mirage

raidwide

wildfire ward - engulfs the arena in fire, except for a triangle safe spot in mid
illuminating glimpse - indicates three knockbacks from the three different sides of the triangle
	position yourself to get knocked from the midpoint of each edge to the opposite corner
	you can also knockback resist two out of the three, and then do the final one properly

noble dawn - creates a large fireball that makes three sets of expanding aoes
	these aoes explode once they reach maximum size, so dodge each set in turn
sublime sunset - jumps behind the fireball, throwing it to the ground in front of her, doing a big proximity aoe
	go to the edge behind her to minimise damage
