inferno - raidwide

ordeal of purgation - summons three concentric rings on the arena with runes at the edges and lines connecting some of the segments
	there will be a fireball from the middle that travels down a line, activating the rune at the end
	these runes are blue triangles that do cone aoes in front of them
	get to the safe spot before you're bound and can't move
ordeal of purgation 2 - same as before, except this time the fireball travels through one of the bent line segments
	make sure you dodge the correct rune

shattering heat - an aoe tankbuster

arch inferno - summons big rotating aoes around the arena and a constant aoe in the middle
hellscathe - three sets of baited puddles under random players
conflagration - a line aoe through the arena
hellscathe
radial flagration - eight cone aoes from the boss
	the rotating aoes keep going during this, so ensure you have enough space to be able to stay in the safe spot until the cones go off

ordeal of purgation 3 - this time the second ring will rotate 45 degrees in the direction of the arrows
	this means the second line segment that the fireball travels through will switch
	for these patterns, the initial rune the fireball is pointing towards (before the rotation) is ALWAYS safe
ordeal of purgation 4 - same as before except the rotation is in the opposite direction

add phase - spawns three flamesent and one greater flamesent
	greater flamesent - casts ghastly torch that stacks up burns on everyone, focus this one down
	flamesent - each one casts a different ability
		ghastly flame - creates puddles around the arena that should be avoided
		ghastly wind - a cone aoe towards a random player
		shattering heat - same as from the boss
blazing rapture - a big transition raidwide

inferno (empowered) - spread aoes on every player that leave a bleed

flamerake - spins a two-pointed arrow that will eventually explode into cascading line aoes

soulscald - line aoes from the boss towards every player, make sure not to overlap with someone else
	these then go back through the entire arena, hopefully there's enough safe space

dualfire - huge cone aoe tankbusters on each tank

sweeping immolation - turns towards a random cardinal or intercardinal, doing a half-room cleave in front of him
	this is paired with either a spread aoe on every player, or a stack on a random player
	he will alternate each time this is cast

ordeal of purgation 6 - same as 4
