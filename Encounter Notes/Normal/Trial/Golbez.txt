azdaja's shadow - empowers the next shadow crescent with a point-blank aoe
shadow crescent + burning shade - a crescent blade cleave paired with a point-blank aoe
	five random players get burning shade spread aoes, so stay out and spread

dragon's descent - gives one player a radial knockback and spawns two towers towards east and west
	the towers will either be a 3-player and 1-player or two 2-player towers
	use the knockback to go into the towers, or use knockback resist and stand in them
double meteor - creates two proximity markers towards two adjacent corners
	at the same time, the player that had the knockback will bait a line aoe from the dragon

gale sphere - spawns four clones towards the cardinals that correspond to the order that the orbs shoot line aoes through the arena
	the clones will either spawn clockwise or anticlockwise
	you can dodge the first two clones' aoes, followed by the last two clones' aoes at once

void stardust - creates two exaflares in two diagonally opposite corners that telegraph eight quick hits before moving
	these travel along the edge in the direction they're pointing, before moving up and going back across the arena
